## Research experience

### Precision measurement of complex cutting tools
Student project leader for research supported by the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars.

- Developed a bi-telecentric 2D vision measurement module with adaptive lighting and calibration accounting for rotation error.
- Developed shape-from-focus 3D topography reconstruction with improved focus measurement and dual-fitting optimization.
- Integrated mechanics, cameras, motors, lighting, and control electronics with MATLAB and C# measurement software.
- Developed a dual-platform vision system with reported measurement accuracy of 3 μm for length and 0.5° for angle.

### Vision-guided robotics for industrial cutting tools
Student project leader for research supported by a National Natural Science Foundation General Program.

- Developed eye-in-hand calibration with a laser tracker and coordinate transformations, achieving reported absolute positioning accuracy of 0.31 mm.
- Combined YOLOv8, binocular vision, hand-eye calibration, and inverse kinematics for tool detection, positioning, tracking, and grasping.
- Integrated an AGV, robot arm, and binocular camera into a vision-guided mobile robot with remote control.

### Composite machining quality prediction
Project leader, National Undergraduate Innovation Project · June 2020 – September 2021.

- Developed a handheld vision inspection device for drilling-exit damage in carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer composites.
- Integrated force, torque, temperature, and vibration sensing.
- Combined sparse autoencoders and Bayesian LSTM models for machining-quality prediction.
