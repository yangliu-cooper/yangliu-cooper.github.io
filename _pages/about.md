I am **Yang LIU (刘洋)**, an engineer at **Huawei**, where I work on industrial vision measurement.

I received my master's degree in Mechanical & Electronic Engineering from [Dalian University of Technology](https://www.dlut.edu.cn/) in June 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Wei Liu. I completed my undergraduate studies at the same university in June 2022.

My research interests span **precision optical metrology, 3D reconstruction, vision-guided robotics, and multi-sensor fusion**. My work connects imaging and measurement algorithms with integrated hardware and software for industrial inspection.

<details class="background-details">
<summary>More about my research background</summary>
<div markdown="1">

- **Precision measurement of complex cutting tools.** Developed 2D vision measurement and shape-from-focus 3D reconstruction methods, adaptive lighting, and integrated measurement software and hardware.
- **Vision-guided robotics.** Worked on eye-in-hand calibration, binocular vision, and deep-learning-based detection for robotic positioning and grasping of industrial cutting tools.
- **Multi-sensor fusion.** Developed inspection and machining-quality prediction methods for carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer composites using vision, process sensing, and deep learning.

See my [CV](/cv/) for education, research experience, and awards.

</div>
